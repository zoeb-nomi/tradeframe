# Broker decision - August 2026

Alpaca and IBKR evaluated Aug 2026; verdict: stay on IndMoney.

Key facts:

- Alpaca margin-only accounts conflict with LRS (India's Liberalised Remittance Scheme).
- Trailing stops unsupported on fractional shares (DAY-TIF only) - breaks the tiered SL ratchet at current fractional position sizes.
- Real wire cost of Rs 3,000-4,400 makes the annual-wire plan roughly Rs 1,500/yr MORE expensive than IndMoney at current scale.
- Breakeven vs IndMoney at roughly Rs 10L annual turnover.
- Watch item: Alpaca GIFT City entry (Zincmoney acquisition, pending IFSCA approval) - would change the wire-cost math entirely.

Full due-diligence document lives in the Claude project ("US Stocks"): Alpaca_Broker_API_Due_Diligence_Aug2026.md.

Revisit trigger: IFSCA approval news, or annual US-stock turnover approaching Rs 10L.
